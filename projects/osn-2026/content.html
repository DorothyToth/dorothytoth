Challenge

After working side-by-side together for years, Adam and I wanted to share our thoughts and experiences around how to achieve great product design from the inside out. Our challenge was corralling our ideas and distilling them down into an organized, concise, and approachable presentation.

Solution

The presentation we produced was the result of months of deep conversations, tons of GitHub issues, countless cups of coffee (or beers, depending on the time of day), and lots of iteration. We organized our thoughts into three principles, added some audience interaction, and a bit of humor.

Ice Breaker!

As the audience took their seats, we displayed a screen with a giant QR code with an x/y axis chart to the right. People could scan the code with their phones and anonymously choose if they were a designer, developer, or other.

With their respectively colored roles displaying in real-time on the screen, they could plot their experience and comfort level working with complementary teams (in other words, designers working with developers, or vice versa).

As more and more people scanned the code, the chart became busy with differently colored dots moving around. About two thirds of the audience were developers, one third were designers, and a few “others” were sprinkled in.

Most people moved their dots into the upper right quadrant of our chart: more experience and higher comfort levels working with complementary teams, which was great to see!

The Golden Key to Badass Products

The best architecture in the world will never solve for bad user experience. To build a truly powerful, dynamic, and efficient platform, the KEY is to align technical architecture with design architecture.

We’ll talk about ways to make this alignment happen, approaches that work (and some that fail), and illustrate organizational wins these practices can achieve.
