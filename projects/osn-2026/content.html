Challenge

The goal of this project was to produce a reorganized, redesigned, and responsive website for the Department of Education.

Solution

The proposed redesign focuses on improving the user experience by simplifying navigation, uncovering important site content, and creating a visually appealing layout.

Ice Breaker!

As the audience took their seats, we displayed a screen with a giant QR code with an x/y axis chart to the right. People could scan the code with their phones and anonymously choose if they were a designer, developer, or other.

With their respectively colored role displaying in real-time on the screen, they could plot their experience working with complementary teams, against their comfort level with complementary teams (in other words, designers working with developers, or vice versa).

As more and more people joined, the chart becaome busy with different colored dots moving around. About one third of the audience were designers, two thirds were developers, and a few “others” were sprinkled in. Most people were in the upper right quadrant of our chart: higher experience, and higher comfort levels, which was great to see!
